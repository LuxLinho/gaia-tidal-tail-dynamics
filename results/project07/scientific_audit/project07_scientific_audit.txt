Project 07 — Scientific Audit
============================================================

L/T circularity
----------------
Homogenized L and T classes are literature-defined leading/trailing-tail classes based on orbital-coordinate information. Therefore the observed 100% L-leading and 100% T-trailing geometric split is definition-linked and is NOT independent physical evidence.

Interpretation
--------------
The extended population is demonstrably non-spherical, but outer-axis orientation is method-sensitive. The intermediate zone is strongly radial, while the outermost morphology rotates away from the local radial stretching axis. Exact outer-tail angles should therefore be treated as descriptive, not as uniquely determined physical tail directions.
